# ShopEase – Frequently Asked Questions

## Q1. What is ShopEase?
ShopEase is an online shopping platform offering electronics, clothing, home appliances, accessories, and lifestyle products.

## Q2. How can I place an order?
Browse products, add your selected items to the cart, enter your delivery address, and complete payment.

## Q3. What payment methods are accepted?
ShopEase supports:
- UPI
- Debit cards
- Credit cards
- Net banking
- Cash on Delivery (where available)

## Q4. How can I track my order?
Log in to your account and visit My Orders to view the latest tracking information.

## Q5. Can I cancel my order?
Yes. Orders can be cancelled before shipment.

## Q6. How long does delivery take?
Delivery generally takes 2–7 business days, depending on the delivery location.

## Q7. Can I return a product?
Eligible products can be returned within 7 days of delivery, subject to the return policy.

## Q8. How long do refunds take?
Refunds generally take 5–7 business days after approval, depending on the payment method.

## Q9. What should I do if I receive a damaged product?
Report the issue within 48 hours and provide photographs to customer support.

## Q10. How can I contact customer support?
Email: support@shopease.example
Phone: 1800-000-1234

## Q11. Do I need an account to shop?
Customers need an account to place and manage orders on ShopEase.

## Q12. Is my personal information safe?
ShopEase uses security measures to protect customer information and restrict unauthorized access.

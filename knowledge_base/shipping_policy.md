# ShopEase – Shipping Policy

## 1. Shipping Locations
ShopEase delivers products across India, including major cities and selected rural locations.

## 2. Shipping Charges
- Standard delivery: ₹50.
- Free delivery on orders above ₹999.
- Express delivery: ₹100 (where available).

## 3. Delivery Time
- Metro cities: 2–4 business days.
- Other cities: 4–7 business days.
- Remote locations: 7–10 business days.

## 4. Order Tracking
Customers receive a tracking link through SMS or email after their order is shipped.

Customers can also track orders through the My Orders section of their ShopEase account.

## 5. Delayed Shipments
If an order is delayed beyond the estimated delivery date, customers can contact support@shopease.example.

## 6. Damaged Packages
Customers should report visibly damaged packages within 48 hours of delivery and provide photographs.

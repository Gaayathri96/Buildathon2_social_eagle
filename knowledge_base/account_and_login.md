# ShopEase – Account and Login Guide

## 1. Creating an Account
Customers can create an account by:
1. Visiting www.shopease.example.
2. Clicking Sign Up.
3. Entering their name and email address.
4. Creating a password.
5. Verifying their email address.

## 2. Logging In
1. Visit the ShopEase website.
2. Click Login.
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Enter your password.
5. Click Sign In.

## 3. Forgot Password
If customers forget their password:
1. Click Forgot Password.
2. Enter their registered email.
3. Follow the password reset link.
4. Create a new password.

## 4. Account Locked
Accounts may be temporarily locked after multiple unsuccessful login attempts.

Customers should wait 30 minutes and try again or contact support.

## 5. Updating Account Details
Customers can update:
- Name
- Phone number
- Delivery address
- Email preferences

## 6. Account Security
- Never share your password.
- Use a strong, unique password.
- Do not share OTPs with anyone.
- Log out when using shared devices.

ShopEase employees will never ask customers to disclose their passwords or OTPs.

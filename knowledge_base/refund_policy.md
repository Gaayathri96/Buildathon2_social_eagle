# ShopEase – Refund and Return Policy

## 1. Return Eligibility
Customers can request a return within 7 days of receiving eligible products.

Products must:
- Be unused and undamaged.
- Have original packaging.
- Include all accessories and tags.

## 2. Non-Returnable Products
The following products cannot normally be returned:
- Personal care products.
- Perishable items.
- Customized products.
- Digital products.

Exceptions may apply to defective, damaged, or incorrectly delivered products.

## 3. Refund Process
1. Log in to your ShopEase account.
2. Navigate to My Orders.
3. Select the product.
4. Click Request Return.
5. Provide the reason for return.

## 4. Refund Timeline
- Refund approval: 2–3 business days after inspection.
- Bank refunds: 5–7 business days after approval.
- Original payment method will be used wherever possible.

## 5. Cancellation Policy
Orders can be cancelled before shipment.

Once shipped, customers must follow the return process.

## 6. Damaged or Incorrect Products
Customers should contact customer support within 48 hours of delivery with photographs and order details.
